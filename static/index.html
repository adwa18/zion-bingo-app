📖 Game Instructions
🎮 How to Play
• Pick a lucky number (1–100) → generates your BINGO card
• Card: 5x5 grid with columns B (1-15), I (16-30), N (31-45), G (46-60), O (61-75)
• Center is FREE (automatically marked)
• Numbers called every 3 seconds (e.g. B-7, O-65)
• Mark matching numbers in the correct column
• Complete a row, column, diagonal, or four corners → tap BINGO!
• Wrong claim = removed from game (no refund)
• Winner takes 90% of the pot

🏆 Winning Patterns
• Any horizontal row
• Any vertical column
• Main diagonal (top-left to bottom-right)
• Anti-diagonal (top-right to bottom-left)
• Four corners (FREE cell counts as a corner)
